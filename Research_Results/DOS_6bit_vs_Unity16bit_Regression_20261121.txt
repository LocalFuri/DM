METHOD
Original indexed native bitmaps 267,268,331,332 were recovered from the
native-dimension view entries 12 (side) and 14 (front) within the archive.
Graphic 332 matches the separately supplied native 92x52 bitmap byte-for-byte.
Using original perspective-specific LUTs and mirrored/clipped geometry,
compared the generated DOS 6-bit sample-coordinate indices with the earlier
16-bit approximate source-coordinate formula documented in the Hook F2 audit:
DOS: step=floor(src_dim*64/dest_dim), source=((step//2)+i*step)>>6
16bit: step=floor(src_dim*1024/dest_dim)*64,
       source=((step//2)+i*step)>>16
No filter, no per-ornament corrections, no RGB conversions.
Every regenerated 6-bit bitmap was byte-for-byte equal to its corresponding
batch BIN file. Indexed palette value 10 is transparent.
