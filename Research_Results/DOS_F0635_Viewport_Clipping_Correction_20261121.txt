Scope:
- Original source: COORD.C F0635_ X720_I34E_I34M_P31J normal layout records 0..8,
  parent records 1 (origin translation) and 9 (224x136 clipping container).
- Original data: user-supplied GRAPHICS.DAT, resource 696, 60 ornament types x 15 view slots.

Correction:
The previous generic geometry prototype incorrectly used the ornament's accumulated
screen coordinates to calculate the parent record 9 clipping rectangle. F0635 instead
positions the clipping rectangle from the IMMEDIATE child layout record and the parent
container dimensions. The viewport clip is [0,224) x [0,136), not translated to
follow the ornament. The new script calculates visible X,Y,Width,Height and source
X/Y trim separately from the underlying uncut bitmap rectangle.
