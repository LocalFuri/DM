Dungeon Master original DOS ornament pixel-rendering audit — 2026-11-21

Scope: original uploaded DUNVIEW.C and DEFS.H, source variant corresponding to the 713-graphic GRAPHICS.DAT. Research-only; NO Unity edits.

SOURCE-PROVEN FACTS
1. DUNVIEW.C contains two 16-entry WALL ORNAMENT PALETTE CHANGE tables in the I34E/I34M/P31J branch:
   D2 / G0199: [0,12,1,3,4,3,6,7,5,9,10,11,0,2,14,13]
   D3 / G0198: [0,0,12,3,4,3,0,6,3,9,10,11,0,1,0,2]
2. At the wall ornament draw path, F0675 receives D2 table for scale 21 and D3 table for scale 14. The far-edge D3 path receives D3 table with scales 30 (horizontal) and 14 (vertical).
3. F0675 passes the palette-change pointer into F0129_VIDEO_BlitShrinkWithPaletteChanges along with native/source and scaled/destination dimensions.
4. Both tables preserve index 10 (10 -> 10). The draw call F0791/F0132 uses transparent color 10. This is an index-based rule, not an RGB-color rule.
5. For comparison, the older P20JA/P20JB variant stores its palette entries multiplied by ten. Do not mix those tables with the selected DOS branch.

EXPERIMENT ERROR IDENTIFIED
The prior DOS_Ornament_Pixel_Sampling_Candidates_20261121.py calls dmcsb.to_pil(), converts to RGBA, samples RGB pixels with edge/center hypotheses, then composites without passing them through either original 16-entry palette-change table. Consequently its output is NOT a faithful simulation of F0129, regardless of the apparent match of a few colored pixels.
The peach-colored area is present in candidate PNG output, absent in DOS reference; its exact source palette index has NOT been established. Do not label it 'transparent index 10' without tracing native palette indices. Equally, recoloring the peach RGB value by hand would not establish authenticity.
The earlier edge/center brown-only pixel scores (76.4%/81.5%) are not evidence of original DOS scaling accuracy: they compared one color after skipping an obligatory palette pass and lacked native index-level pixel data.
