IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS
- This verifies the selected source branch and validates the prototype calculations against layout parent records; it does not establish pixel-for-pixel DOS rendering equivalence.
- ReDMCSB F0635 supports additional record kinds 10..18 and shift modes; those are not implemented in the prototype. The 900 audited wall ornament zones use record kinds 0/5/7 and share parent record types 1/9. The limited path is sufficient for these simple layout records only.
- Bitmap shrinking, transparent palette, and actual wall occlusion are not implemented in the offline geometry prototype.
- Validation with real DOS captures is required before Unity integration; do not introduce ornament-specific corrections.
