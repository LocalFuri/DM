## New source-level finding
ReDMCSB source discussion cites the original renderer lookup:
`ZONE = C1004_ZONE_WALL_ORNAMENT + CoordinateSet * 15 + ViewWallIndex`.
This identifies 15 perspective zones/category (rather than the 13 rows in ScummVM's inherited common wall-ornament coordinate sets).
Source: https://www.dungeon-master.com/forum/viewtopic.php?start=30&t=29805

ReDMCSB reverse-engineering discussion also cites engine 3.x scale factors D2 21/32 and D3 14/32. Treat as documented ratio but not yet fully validated integer rounding/positioning rule.

This is evidence for a shared horizontal rule, not proof of a complete generic rectangle algorithm. Do not implement ornament-specific overrides.

## Additional research — 20.11.2026 18:59:08 CET

The original DOS/engine 3.x layout uses LAY1 resource 696 with a parent-relative zone alignment system, not just fixed destination rectangles. ReDMCSB names the resolver F0635_CalculateAlignment. Its documented alignment commands include CENTER (0), corners (1-4), edge centers (5-8), DIMENSIONS (9), and margin-alignment modes (10-18). The calculation follows parent zones until a root parent 0.

Wall ornament zone lookup: 1004 + coordinate_set*15 + view_wall_index. The 15 zones per class must be decoded and their parent-relative alignment resolved. The exact raw zone records/parent chains for Hook F2, Hook F3 and Manacles F2 have NOT yet been extracted, so the original DOS final X/Y/W/H is NOT proven.

The source audit at https://github.com/levincem/dungeonMaster-codex/blob/main/docs/ORIGINAL_DATA_AUDIT.md independently reports resource 696 as a 9160-byte/4580-word composition/layout container; this third-party decode is partial and is not a substitute for checking the exact original executable.

Currently supported shared hypothesis: F2 width=floor(sourceWidth*21/32), horizontal center x=(224-width)/2: Hook 28->18, x103; Manacles 92->60, x82. Height and Y positioning not established; current Manacles 60x35 at (82,40) is a regression target, not an ornament-specific rule to bake in. Hook F3 logical 32-column sampler not generalized.

Next: decode parent-relative LAY1 zones for the 15 view indices and determine exact height/vertical anchor; offline tests must reproduce all three reference cases before changing Unity. No code/artwork changed.
