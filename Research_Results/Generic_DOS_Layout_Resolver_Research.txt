LIMITS / WHAT WE DID NOT ESTABLISH
- LAY1 centers are validated data; they do not prove the final on-screen rectangle without full original F0791/F0635 drawing logic.
- The drawing routine may pass different logical/stored bitmap dimensions or apply clipping. Hook F3 logical padding and 4px Y mismatch still require the actual source path.
- Fixed distance scale factors (21/32 and 14/32) are documented externally, but their integer rounding, source stride, and destination height must be verified for this DOS version.
- Do not replace approved Hook F2/F3 or Manacles provisional F2 with calculated coordinates until resolved.
- No ornament-name special cases, manual coordinate fixes, or artwork edits.

DELIVERABLE
DOS_Ornament_Layout_Calculator.py is a research-only generic diagnostic. It accepts resource 696 binary and a coordinate set and view index, optionally a KNOWN destination width and height.
