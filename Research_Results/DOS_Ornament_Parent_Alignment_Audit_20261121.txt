COORD.C lines 1813-2159:
  F0635_ resolves LAYOUT_RECORD with F0634_GetLayoutRecord; applies parent-relative offsets, special 10..18 margin alignment rules, bounds and clipping.
  For alignment record type 0 (CENTER):
       left = effectiveX - ((width + 1) >> 1)
       top  = effectiveY - ((height + 1) >> 1)
  This formula holds for centered alignment only; other modes 1..8 have different formulas.
  Parent resolution can alter effectiveX and effectiveY; viewport clipping may alter top/left and visible width/height.
  For PC X508, rectangle uses inclusive left/right/top/bottom fields, and positive clip excess is returned to the blitter as source offsets.
